World of Frozen

March 2026

I was given a unique, open brief to make something to support the launch of World of Frozen at Disneyland Paris from Disney+.

As a cross-functional collaboration, we had to get the messaging just right, whilst still doing something entertaining and magical for our Frozen fans.

So we created a faux-OOH billboard that celebrated the different ways you can experience Frozen - and followed it up with a lovely little piece where Disney+ characters sing Let It Go.
