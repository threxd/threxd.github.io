LEGO Star Wars

May 2016

🚨Promax winner alert!🚨

These idents were some of the most fun I had in my partnerships days.

We got to create a mini LEGO Star Wars gag to support the release of The Force Awakens and the Star Wars Rebels TV Show. With wonderful animation again from Blue Zoo.

I’ll never forget the late-night-brainstorm delirium that lead to the banana gag. It was completely stupid and I knew immediately we were going to win a Promax for it.

And I was right!
