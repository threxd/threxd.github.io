Who I Am and What I Do

Good Morning

If you don’t know me, I’m a big-dreaming creative who loves zeroing in on emotional truths. If you do know me, you needn’t bother reading all this tbh.

Most of my experience comes from Disney — and there’s a shedload of it. From cutting my teeth promo-producing for channels, through agency-style pitching and developing for clients in Partnerships, to shaping 360 campaigns and brand creative for Disney+.

I’ve worn a lot of different creative hats, both in and out of the mouse house — I edit, write, direct and design. I love exploring uncharted territory and collect skills like Thanos does infinity stones (so on-brand, me). And all that experience means I’m super adaptable and able to conceptualise in a truly audience-first, emotionally engaging way.
